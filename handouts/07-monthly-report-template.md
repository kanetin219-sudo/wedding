**Supabaseクエリ**（代行者用・参考）：
```sql
SELECT
  count(*) AS total_posts,
  round(avg(views)::numeric, 1) AS avg_views,
  percentile_cont(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY views) AS median_views,
  round(avg(likes)::numeric, 1) AS avg_likes,
  round(100.0 * sum(likes+replies+reposts)::numeric / NULLIF(sum(views), 0), 2) AS engagement_rate_pct
FROM {クライアント別テーブル名}
WHERE posted_at >= '{今月1日}' AND posted_at < '{翌月1日}';
```
